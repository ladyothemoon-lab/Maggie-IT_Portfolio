# AGHF Risk Assessment

## Risks Identified

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|------------|--------|------------|
| Malware | Medium | High | Antivirus, Updates |
| Ransomware | Medium | High | Backups, Security Training |
| Unauthorized Access | Medium | High | MFA, Password Policies |
| Data Loss | Low | High | Backups |
| Hardware Failure | Medium | Medium | Replacement Plan |
| WiFi Breach | Low | High | WPA3, Segmentation |

## Risk Management Strategy

AGHF will reduce risk through:

- Security awareness
- Device hardening
- VLAN segmentation
- Strong authentication
- Regular backups
- Software updates
